About Acerca de

The portfolio tool we wished existed.

La herramienta de portafolio que deseábamos que existiera.

Plain-language investing, built for people, not finance professionals.

Inversión en lenguaje simple, hecha para personas, no para profesionales de las finanzas.

FolioSense is a free, AI-powered tool that helps everyday investors understand what their stock portfolio is actually doing. You upload a CSV or Excel file from your broker, and in a few seconds you get a risk score, a sector breakdown, your best and worst performers, and a chat advisor that answers questions about your holdings in plain language.

No account. No spreadsheet wrangling. No finance degree required.

Why we built it

Most investing tools are built for people who already speak the language of finance. They throw around terms like beta, Sharpe ratio, and sector concentration as if everyone learned them in school. Most of us did not.

We kept seeing the same thing: people who had done the hard part (actually started investing) but had no clear way to answer a simple question, "is my portfolio okay?" They could see a list of tickers and a total balance, but not whether they were dangerously concentrated in one sector, taking on more risk than they realized, or holding positions that quietly dragged everything down.

FolioSense exists to answer that question in language a normal person can read, for free.

What FolioSense does

  • Instant analysis. Upload your holdings and get a full breakdown in seconds: risk score, sector distribution, and your top and worst performers.
  • AI chat advisor. Ask anything about your portfolio in plain English (or Spanish) and get answers based on your actual holdings.
  • Visual breakdowns. Interactive pie charts, bar charts, scatter plots, and a risk-return view, so you can see where your money really is.
  • Learning built in. A glossary and a growing blog explain the concepts behind the numbers, one plain-language term at a time.

Who is behind FolioSense

FolioSense is an independent project built and maintained by a small team that cares about making investing less intimidating. We are not a bank, a brokerage, or a fund. We do not manage anyone's money. We are investors ourselves who got tired of tools that explained nothing, so we built the one we wanted to use.

How we keep it free

FolioSense is supported by non-intrusive advertising and by referral partnerships with platforms we genuinely think are worth recommending. When you sign up for a partner through one of our links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend products we would use ourselves, and we say clearly when a link is a referral link. There are no paywalls and no premium tiers. The analyzer is free for everyone, forever.

What FolioSense is not

FolioSense is an educational and informational tool. It is not financial advice, and it is not a substitute for a licensed financial advisor. The analysis and AI responses are generated automatically and can be incomplete or wrong. You are always responsible for your own investment decisions. Investing involves risk, including the possible loss of money you put in.

Por qué lo creamos

La mayoría de las herramientas de inversión están hechas para personas que ya hablan el idioma de las finanzas. Usan términos como beta, ratio de Sharpe o concentración por sector como si todos los hubiéramos aprendido en la escuela. La mayoría no lo hicimos.

Veíamos lo mismo una y otra vez: personas que ya habían hecho lo más difícil (empezar a invertir) pero no tenían una forma clara de responder una pregunta sencilla, "¿está bien mi portafolio?". Podían ver una lista de tickers y un saldo total, pero no si estaban peligrosamente concentradas en un sector, asumiendo más riesgo del que creían, o cargando con posiciones que arrastraban todo hacia abajo.

FolioSense existe para responder esa pregunta en un lenguaje que una persona normal pueda leer, gratis.
